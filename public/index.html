OBS Setup
  1. 1
    In OBS, add a Browser Source to your scene.
  2. 2
    Set the URL to: http://localhost:3000/overlay.html
  3. 3
    Set dimensions to 1920 × 1080 and position it at the top-left of your canvas. The centre is fully transparent — only the side panels are visible.
  4. 4
    Check "Shutdown source when not visible" and "Refresh browser when scene becomes active".
  5. 5
    Share the Player 1 / Player 2 QR codes above with your opponents — they connect via their phone on the same Wi-Fi.
Tip: You can resize the browser source to any height — the overlay is fully transparent outside the panels.
